What's included

Lead gen, treated as infrastructure.

Every engagement runs the same shape: audit, plan, build, manage. Tap any stage for detail.

Funnel Audit

A structured review of your current funnel: where prospects drop off, where spend is wasted, attribution against real outcomes, and a look at your brand guide so everything recommended stays on-brand, plus what a fixed version looks like.

Enrollment Growth Playbook

A tactical, ROI-modeled growth playbook covering timeline, budget, and audience journey mapping, built to hold up under stakeholder review.

Funnel Build & Media Buying

Full-funnel build from inquiry to conversion, including the messaging, ad creative, landing pages, email copy, and design, built to fit your brand guide and any campaigns already running. Paid search, paid social, and CRM-driven nurture bought against real acquisition cost targets, not just impressions.

Reporting

Cost per acquisition weighed against real gross and net revenue, attribution by channel, and reporting frameworks built for a room that isn't fluent in marketing metrics.

Compliance & CRM Alignment

Messaging and offer structures built with the compliance requirements of your industry in mind, whether that's financial aid disclosures and accreditation for higher ed or donor and membership regulations for non-profits, plus making sure your CRM and marketing stack are actually talking to each other.

Ongoing Management

Regular optimization and reporting once campaigns are live, not launched once and left alone.
