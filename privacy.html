Who this covers

This policy explains how Spaso Marketing ("I," "me," or "my") collects, uses, and shares personal information when you visit spasomarketing.com, fill out the contact form, or book a consultation. Spaso Marketing is a one-person consultancy based in the United States.

What I collect

I collect only what you choose to give me:

  • Contact form: your name, email address, organization (optional), and any details you add (optional).
  • Booking a consultation: your name and email address, and any answers you give on the booking page, which is run by Google Calendar.
  • Email: anything you send to hello@spasomarketing.com.

My hosting provider and the services below may also automatically log technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you visit.

How I use it

  • To reply to your inquiry and follow up about lead generation consulting.

  • To run and improve this website.

I do not sell your personal information. I do not use it to send unrelated marketing, and I do not share it with anyone for their own marketing purposes.

I may also disclose information if required by law or to protect my legal rights.

If I do, those tools may use cookies or similar technologies, and I will update this policy before or when they are added.

How long I keep it

You can ask me to tell you what information I hold about you, correct it, or delete it. To do so, email hello@spasomarketing.com. I will respond within a reasonable time, and generally within 45 days.
